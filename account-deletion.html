Delete Your SLAP RPS Account or Data

Last updated: 28 September 2026

For SLAP RPS, published by Bui Phi Quan / S Games Studios, Viet Nam, you can request deletion without reinstalling or opening the game.

Request deletion by email

Email slapduelrockpaperscissor@gmail.com with the subject SLAP RPS account deletion. Say that you want your game account and associated data deleted. Include your game player ID if available, your platform (Android or iOS), and the sign-in method you used. If you do not know your player ID, say so; we will help identify the account and verify ownership before acting.

You can instead request deletion of particular personal information, such as a support conversation, without asking to close the whole account. Describe which information you mean. We may need to explain if a requested deletion would prevent a game feature from working.

Do not include your password, authentication token or full payment card details. Only send information relevant to identifying the account. A purchase receipt is needed only if it helps resolve account ownership or a purchase question; unrelated personal details can be removed.

Deletion from the game

In versions that include Settings → Delete Account, open that control, read the consequences and confirm only for the account you intend to delete. Keep a network connection while the request is processed. A pending message or connection error is not a completion confirmation; follow the game's retry instruction or contact support. If your version does not offer the control or you cannot sign in, use the email route above.

What the request covers

A full account-deletion request covers the Unity game identity and linked game-account associations, cloud progress and purchase/claim records held in that progress, Arena publication and account records, and associated recovery or staged game saves held by us. It does not delete your Apple or Google account.

Deleting the game account means losing its cloud progress, balances, inventory, rewards and game entitlements. Deletion is not a refund and does not cancel a separate store obligation. Contact Apple or Google for store payment or refund matters. If you have an unfinished purchase or an account-recovery operation, contact support or complete it before using an in-game deletion flow that asks you to settle it.

What is separate

  • Other devices and backups: remote deletion does not physically erase an offline device or an operating-system backup. Remove local app data from your other devices as well. Do not re-upload old saves after asking to delete the account.
  • Deletion security record: after game-data deletion completes, a hashed game-account identifier and deletion status can remain without automatic expiry to block old authenticated requests from recreating deleted data. This record has no progress, purchase history, sign-in token, installation identifier or activity timestamp; it is not an account backup.
  • Provider records: Apple, Google, advertising providers and Unity may hold separate payment, advertising, security or service records under their own policies. Deleting the game account is not a claim that all of those independent records are instantly erased. We handle or route requests for provider-held data as appropriate to the provider's role.
  • Support correspondence and exceptions: specify whether you also want your support correspondence removed. Limited records may need to remain for a legal obligation or an unresolved dispute. We explain any applicable exception and its retention basis when responding, rather than treating it as permission to keep all gameplay data.

Confirmation and help

We may ask proportionate questions to verify ownership so that another person cannot delete your account. We will respond about the request, any information needed, and its outcome within the time required by applicable law. Do not treat sending an email or pressing a button as proof that deletion is already complete. If a request cannot be completed immediately, we explain the status and next step.

Uninstalling SLAP RPS, signing out or removing Apple/Google authorization alone does not delete the game account and cloud records. Read the privacy policy and account help, or contact slapduelrockpaperscissor@gmail.com.
