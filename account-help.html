SLAP RPS Accounts and Saves

Last updated: 28 September 2026

SLAP RPS is published by Bui Phi Quan / S Games Studios, Viet Nam. Account and purchase support: slapduelrockpaperscissor@gmail.com.

Playing as a guest

You can play PvE using the save on your device without linking Apple or Google. Connected Arena features can create a Unity player account even if you have not linked a sign-in provider. An unlinked guest account may be impossible to recover after its device data or credentials are lost. Deleting the app is not a request to erase its cloud records.

Linking and recovering progress

Open Settings and choose an available Google or Apple sign-in button. Apple is offered on supported iOS builds; Google is offered on configured Android and iOS builds. An unavailable integration is shown as unavailable. A successful sign-in links identity; a cloud backup is complete only when the game confirms that your save is synced.

Link the provider while using the progress you want to keep. When a provider is already linked to another game account, the game offers recovery of that account. Accounts are not combined because they use the same email address. To recover on another platform, use a provider available there that was linked to the same game account.

Normal saving happens on your device first. Linked accounts sync automatically at suitable menu points when connected. Settings shows sync status and the last confirmed sync time. Connection failures leave local PvE available; they do not prove a backup succeeded.

Choosing between different saves

If both this device and the cloud have changed since their last shared save, Choose your progress compares the two. Select Use this device or Use cloud save. Your selected progress replaces the other active save. Progress and purchases are not combined. A newer displayed time does not automatically win.

You can choose either save even if the other contains completed purchases. The selected save keeps its own wallet, inventory, ownership and purchase history. The game does not add rewards or replay purchases from the discarded branch. If separate devices made separate purchases and your selected save is missing something you bought, contact support with the store order information; support handles that case separately.

Closing the choice leaves this device playable and pauses the unresolved replacement. If either save changes while you are reviewing it, the game asks you to review again. Finish any active fight or unresolved purchase before switching saves. A previously confirmed load with an uncertain network result must finish before you can choose its opposite.

Recovery copies are kept separately to help recover interrupted operations. There is no general in-game browser for restoring any historical save.

Purchases, privacy and deletion

Use the store's supported restore flow for eligible permanent purchases. Spent consumables are not an unlimited restorable balance. For missing purchases, email support with the store name, order identifier, approximate purchase time and a description of the missing item. Do not send passwords or full card details.

Read our privacy policy or follow the account deletion steps. Signing out, unlinking a provider, choosing a save and deleting an account are different actions.
