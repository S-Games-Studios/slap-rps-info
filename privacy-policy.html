SLAP RPS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

SLAP RPS is published by Bui Phi Quan, an individual developer in Viet Nam, under the publishing name S Games Studios. In this policy, “we” means this publisher. Contact us about privacy at slapduelrockpaperscissor@gmail.com.

This policy covers the game, its account and online features, advertisements, purchases, support, and these information pages. Feature availability depends on your version, device, connection and sign-in configuration.

Information we process and why

  • Local progress: your game progress, Gold and tickets, inventory and upgrades, selected builds and cosmetics, rewards, claims, purchase records, ad reward records and game preferences are saved on your device so that you can continue playing. Local play does not require a social sign-in.
  • Game accounts and cloud saves: Unity Authentication creates a player identifier when you use connected account or Arena features. If you choose Apple or Google sign-in, that provider supplies an identity token to authenticate or link your game account. Identity tokens can contain provider identifiers and profile claims, including an email address if supplied by the provider. The game uses the token for authentication; it does not copy your provider profile or token into your gameplay save. Linked accounts can back up progress to Unity Cloud Save. Backup and recovery records include progress, purchase identifiers, entitlements, reward history and save times. Installation identifiers and revision records coordinate devices and prevent conflicting online updates.
  • Arena: Unity processes your published character/build, selected equipment and abilities, progression values, match records, points, reward eligibility and request times to find opponents, run asynchronous matches, award results and prevent duplicate or abusive requests. Other players can encounter your published build. The game displays its in-game alias, character appearance, HP, attack and relative progression; battle data includes the build required to run the match. The current client supplies the alias “Brawler,” rather than your Apple or Google name. There is no player chat or direct device-to-device match connection in this game.
  • Purchases: Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment through Unity In-App Purchasing. The game receives product, transaction, purchase status and quantity information to deliver purchases, prevent duplicate delivery and restore supported ownership. The saved purchase ledger includes transaction hashes, product and offer identifiers, entitlements and unresolved checkout records. We do not receive full payment card details from this flow. Unity's purchasing services can also process account, installation, session, device, country, purchase and diagnostic information for commerce operation and measurement. See Unity IAP privacy information.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob supplies rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Its SDK can process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers where available, ad views and interactions, app interactions and performance/diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Ad personalization depends on applicable privacy choices and provider settings. See Google's advertising data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.
  • Service and support information: Unity services process account and connection information needed to authenticate requests, store progress and operate online features. Service providers can process technical and diagnostic information to maintain their services. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to handle your request.

The current game does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Unity Ads. Ad and purchasing SDKs have their own measurement functions; the absence of a separate gameplay analytics SDK does not mean those providers process no usage information.

Services and recipients

We use Unity Authentication, Cloud Code and Cloud Save to authenticate players, operate online game features and store cloud progress. Unity also supplies the purchasing SDK and optional shared Remote Config services. Read Unity's privacy policy.

Apple and Google authenticate the sign-in you select and operate their respective app stores. Read Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy. Google AdMob and its advertising partners process advertising information under their own policies and the choices presented in the advertising privacy form. Our support address uses Gmail, which processes correspondence under Google's policies.

We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to investigate fraud, protect accounts, or defend legal rights. We do not provide other players with your sign-in token, email address or payment details.

Advertising choices

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether an advertising privacy message is required before requesting ads. Open Settings → Ad Privacy Options to revisit the available form. Some versions label the control Ad Privacy / Retry when no form is currently required or the privacy check needs to be retried. Available choices depend on your location and Google's configured message.

On iOS, you can allow or deny the system tracking permission and change it in the device's tracking settings. Android provides advertising-ID controls in device settings. Declining personalized advertising or tracking does not necessarily remove all ads or all processing needed for ad delivery, measurement and security. Contact us if you need help exercising an applicable advertising opt-out. Do not assume an advertising choice also changes account, purchasing or support processing.

Storage and retention

Local saves remain on your device until replaced or removed through the game's supported deletion flow or device storage controls. Device backups can retain separate copies.

Connected account records and cloud progress remain stored until deleted; the game does not automatically close an account simply because it is inactive. Cloud storage can contain the current save, recovery copies and historical or staged save records. Choosing between two saves replaces active progress; it is not an account-deletion request and does not immediately erase all recovery records.

After the game-data deletion process completes, a limited security record can remain: a hashed game-account identifier and deletion status. It contains no saved progress, purchase history, sign-in token, installation identifier or activity timestamp. This record has no automatic expiry; its purpose is to prevent old authenticated requests from recreating the deleted account's game data. It is not used to restore progress or target advertising.

Provider service records have separate retention rules. Unity describes default retention until deletion for Authentication and Cloud Save, and retention of its Cloud Code personal data for up to 30 days in its Cloud Code privacy information. These provider periods do not describe the lifetime of the game's saved profiles. Ad, store and support-provider records are governed by their own purposes and retention policies. We cannot promise that deleting a game account immediately deletes records independently held by Apple, Google or Unity.

Support correspondence remains in the support mailbox unless removed while handling the request or a later deletion request. Where a legal requirement or an unresolved dispute requires limited information to be kept, we explain the relevant exception when responding to your request. We do not promise a fixed expiry for every provider's backup or security record.

Your account and privacy rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your information by emailing slapduelrockpaperscissor@gmail.com. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to receive a copy, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, opt out of certain advertising uses, or complain to a data protection authority. If you believe a request was handled incorrectly, reply to the same address asking for a review.

We may ask for information needed to identify the correct game account and verify your request. Never send your password, sign-in token or full payment card details. See account deletion for the request steps and consequences. Uninstalling the game or disconnecting a sign-in provider does not itself delete cloud records.

Children and international processing

The game is intended for players 13 and older, and is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information can contact us to request review and deletion. Players who are minors should involve a parent or guardian where local rules require it. The 13+ audience description is not a statement that every teenager can independently consent to personalized advertising.

Our publisher is in Viet Nam. The service providers described above operate internationally, so information may be processed outside your country. Their privacy policies describe their locations, applicable transfer safeguards and ways to contact them. Contact us with questions about the processing of your game account.

Information pages, security and changes

These static information pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, sign-in form or account database. Email links open your email application. When you visit a copy hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs the visitor's IP address for security; see GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.

We use protected local saves and authenticated service requests to reduce unauthorized access and accidental loss. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. We update this page when practices change and show the revision date above. Questions can be sent to Bui Phi Quan / S Games Studios at slapduelrockpaperscissor@gmail.com.
